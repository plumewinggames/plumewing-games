## 3. High-Converting Content Frameworks

### A. The 3-Second Viral Hook Matrix (TikTok / Reels)
Every short-form video must arrest scrolling in the first 3 seconds:
1. **The Visual Pattern Interrupt:** Split-screen showing puffy, unmanageable hair on the left, being transformed with a single comb glide on the right.
2. **The Controversial / Secret Angle:** *"Salons charge $350 for this treatment and pray you never find out it's just this one organic bottle."*
3. **The Relatable Agitation:** *"If your hair expands like a lion's mane every time you step outside, watch this before you burn your hair again."*
4. **The Sensory Satisfying Hook:** Slow-motion comb dropping effortlessly through liquid glass hair with satisfying ASMR sound.

### B. Educational Carousel Framework (Instagram Authority)
* **Slide 1 (Hook Cover):** High-contrast headline addressing a deep hair frustration.
* **Slide 2 (The Science in Simple Words):** Why traditional treatments use formaldehyde and why 0% formaldehyde nanoplasty is fundamentally different.
* **Slide 3 (The Step-by-Step Magic):** 1-Step application overview (Wash $\rightarrow$ Apply $\rightarrow$ Rinse $\rightarrow$ Flat-iron).
* **Slide 4 (The Temperature Secret):** Clear guidance by hair type (Fine/Blonde vs. Thick/Coarse).
* **Slide 5 (The 4-Month Result):** Before & After proof.
* **Slide 6 (Call to Action):** *"Comment 'GLASS' to get the step-by-step PDF guide + discount link in your DMs."*
